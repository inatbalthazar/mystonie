# Pages & navigation

Mobile-first ([ADR 0050](../decisions/0050-mobile-first-nav-island.md)): the phone is the main screen. Signed in, every page gets the **nav island**, a floating bar at the bottom at every width: **Home · Collection · ➕ · Feed · Me** ([ADR 0053](../decisions/0053-feed-tab-stats-in-me.md)). ➕ opens quick add (in place on the collection). Feed stays lit on the community pages and the Journal, and gets a coral dot when someone stamped your finish or followed you since you last opened it ([ADR 0054](../decisions/0054-feed-dot-reel-reminders.md)). Me is your own album with Settings, and its second tab is Stats. New accounts also get the getting-started checklist's round progress button just above the island, bottom right, until they finish or skip it ([ADR 0056](../decisions/0056-getting-started-button.md)). The header keeps only the logo (and Sign in when signed out). Every route supports a locale prefix via next-intl (`/` = English default, `/th/...` = Thai). See [i18n](../architecture/i18n.md).

# ADR 0059: The Atlas: countries you've been to and countries your stories come from, on one world map

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-01

## Context
The owner (2026-10-01) asked for a travel section: type a country (or a province), and see the places you've been highlighted on a map. Travel had been "not core" and gated ([open questions](../open-questions.md), [ADR 0036](0036-expansion-features-before-launch.md)); the owner gave the go after agreeing to this plan.

What shaped it:
- **Mystonie is about stories you finish.** A travel log on its own pulls away from that. A map of where your movies, series and manga come from needs no typing and makes the map about stories too.
- **Global, nothing country-specific** ([ADR 0007](0007-english-first-global.md)). One country's provinces would break that. Every country's regions (about 4,500 shapes, several MB) is too much for a first version.
- **Privacy.** Where you've lived says where you're from. The bio left nationality out on purpose ([ADR 0057](0057-profile-bio.md)).
- **Free, small, no new services.** No Mapbox or Google Maps keys, no new packages ([ADR 0006](0006-single-nextjs-app.md)).

## Decision
**Name: "Atlas"**, the collection's fourth tab (`/collection/atlas`) after Watch · Read · Play. "Travel" sounds like a booking app. "Passport stamps" would clash with the Journal's Stamps.

**Where it lives:** in the collection, because places are collected like stories (the owner, 2026-10-01). It was first built as Me's third tab (`/atlas`, after Album and Stats) and moved the same day, before any deploy, so no redirect. It is its own server render: its tab is a link, and on the Atlas page the Watch, Read and Play tabs link back to `/collection?shelf=…`. The tabs show even on an empty collection, so the Atlas can come first. The nav island lights Collection.

**Two layers on one map:**
- **Been:** countries you've been to (coral), lived in (darker) or want to go to (hatched). You add them by tapping the map or by searching for a name in your language or English (codes and UK/USA work too). A tap on a status saves it at once, so a country is 2 taps away. The first-visit year is optional and only for a visit.
- **Stories:** the countries of the titles you're watching, reading or have finished (not Want to watch). Read from the cached catalog body:
  - TMDB `origin_country`, else `production_countries`, for movies and series.
  - AniList `countryOfOrigin` for manga.
  - Books and games have none, and the page says so.
  - A co-production counts for each of its countries. More stories show in a stronger teal.
- **Both:** "Been there, watched that" counts the visited countries that also have stories. A country's sheet shows its stories (posters), linking to their pages.

**Country level here.** Regions come for every country, loaded one country at a time: built the same day, [ADR 0060](0060-atlas-regions.md).

**The map is our own SVG, no package:**
- `scripts/atlas-map.mjs` builds it from Natural Earth's 1:50m countries (public domain), with the Natural Earth I projection, Douglas–Peucker simplification and relative path encoding.
- The output is `src/components/atlas/world-map-data.ts` (about 86 KB), its own chunk loaded with the Atlas.
- Antarctica is left out. Overseas parts with their own ISO code (French Guiana, Réunion, Svalbard, …) are split from their country.
- About 90 small countries also get a dot, so they can be seen and tapped.
- Continent chips zoom the viewBox.
- Continents come from the UN M49 regions (via the lukes/ISO-3166-Countries-with-Regional-Codes list), with the Americas split into North and South: `src/core/continents.ts`, generated by the same script.
- Colours are theme tokens (`--atlas-*`), light and dark.

**Private by default:**
- `profiles.atlas_public` (off) is "Show my Atlas on my profile", on the Atlas page and in Settings → Privacy.
- When on, and the profile is public and not blocked, visitors see the Been layer's map and counts on the album. RLS enforces this.
- Want-to-go countries never show.

**Share my Atlas:**
- An Atlas card (the Scratch map template, story and feed, plus the sticker): the visited countries coloured in with the card's accent, the map zoomed to them (the whole world when they spread far), the countries by name on the story size, and the numbers.
- The server saves an Atlas card only when its countries are exactly the owner's visited places.

## Consequences
- **New:**
  - The table `places` and `profiles.atlas_public`, with the card kind `atlas`, in `20261016090000_stage4_atlas.sql` (pgTAP `stage4_atlas.test.sql`). **Apply it to the remote project before deploying.**
  - `src/core/atlas.ts` (with tests) and `src/core/continents.ts`.
  - `src/data/atlas.ts` and `POST /api/places`.
  - `src/components/atlas/*` and `src/cards/templates/atlas.tsx`.
  - Analytics `place_saved` `{status}`, never the country.
- **The Stories layer is only as good as the cache.** Titles cached before TMDB's details body was kept (seeded or very old rows) show no country until they are refreshed.
- **Not built:**
  - "Before you go" (TMDB's discover by origin country for a want-to-go country).
  - Flags (emoji flags don't render on Windows).
  - The Stories layer on the public album.
  - Places in the collection export (the export is the collection's titles and logs).
- **Disputed borders** follow Natural Earth's default point of view. Names come from `Intl` in the viewer's language. Land without an ISO code (Somaliland, Northern Cyprus) is drawn but never lights up.

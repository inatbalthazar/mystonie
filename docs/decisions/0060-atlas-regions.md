# ADR 0060: The Atlas's regions: every country's states and provinces, on each country's own page

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-01

## Context
The owner asked for the province or state level of the Atlas, with a good UX. [ADR 0059](0059-atlas.md) kept the Atlas at country level and said regions would come for every country, loaded one country at a time, not one country's provinces ([ADR 0007](0007-english-first-global.md)).

What shaped it:
- **Natural Earth's admin-1** (1:10m, public domain) covers every country, but at the level its sources use. The UK is 232 council areas, France 101 departments and Italy 110 provinces, while travellers think "Scotland", "Brittany", "Tuscany".
- **Names in two languages.** Natural Earth has no Thai names, and its English ones are sometimes local ("Kyōto", "Washington" for the District of Columbia).
- **Small screens.** Thailand has 77 provinces in 330 px, and the US has Alaska and Hawaii far from the rest.
- **Free and small, no packages** ([ADR 0006](0006-single-nextjs-app.md)).

## Decision
**Where:** each country has its own page, `/collection/atlas/<cc>` (Collection lit), opened from the country's sheet. The sheet has a row "Provinces · 6 of 77" with a bar. Countries drawn as a dot on the world map (Singapore, Andorra, Malta) have no regions.

**The page:**
- **Progress on top:** "6/77 provinces you've been to", the share in %, and a bar.
- **The country's map:**
  - A tap marks a region or unmarks it, saved at once.
  - A zoom button doubles the map inside its frame, which scrolls natively, for small regions such as Bangkok's neighbours.
  - The region just changed is outlined for a moment.
- **A line under the map:** "Marked Chiang Mai · Undo", for mis-taps.
- **Every region as a chip** (sorted in the viewer's language), with search. This is the accessible way to the same regions, and the map is one labelled image.
- **"Share my Japan":** a card.

**Marking a region puts its country on the Atlas** as Been there, or moves it there from Want to go. A visit or a home stays as it is. Making a country Want to go, or taking it off, clears its regions. With regions marked, the sheet asks first, inline next to the button tapped. No region status other than "been there": a lived-in country stays country level, and a region is one tap.

**The data, generated by `scripts/atlas-regions.mjs`, no packages:**
- **Merges:** the UK into its four nations, and France, Italy, Spain and the Philippines into their regions. Slovenia's and Latvia's municipalities go into statistical regions. Features sharing an ISO code merge too (Ireland's Dublin, Madagascar's provinces).
- **How merging works:** shared edges cancel out, and what is left is walked back into rings. Holes (Berlin in Brandenburg) are kept with `evenodd`.
- **Projection:** per country, equirectangular at the middle latitude. Countries across the date line (Russia, the Aleutians, Fiji) are made whole.
- **Insets:** far-off parts go in dashed frames under the main map. These are Alaska, Hawaii, the Canaries, the Azores and Madeira, Okinawa, the Galápagos, San Andrés and the Chatham Islands. Each frame is between a fifth and a third of the map. Far-flung bits of other regions (Easter Island, Ogasawara) are left off.
- **Tiny regions:** too small to draw, they get a dot with a finger-sized target.
- **Size:** 150 countries and 2,940 regions, in `public/atlas/regions/v1/<CC>.json`. That is 1.3 MB in all, at most 35 KB for one country (Canada), and only the opened country loads.
- **Ids:** ISO 3166-2 where Natural Earth has a valid one, else `CC-Z<Natural Earth id>`, and `CC-<6 letters>` for the merged statistical regions. `src/core/regions.ts` lists every country's ids and kind (state, province, prefecture, …), for the server to check and count. A rerun stops if an id it wrote before would disappear, because ids are stored.
- **Names:** English and Thai from Wikidata (CC0). A single feature uses its own Wikidata item. Merged regions use the item with their ISO code. ISO codes alone aren't trusted for one feature: where ISO renumbered (Iran, Vietnam) they name another place. "Province", "Prefecture", "Governorate" and "State" are dropped ("Kyoto"), and so are "จังหวัด", "รัฐ" and "แคว้น", unless two regions would then share a name. 144 regions have no Thai name and show English.

**Privacy:** `place_regions` follows `places` (`private.is_public_atlas`). It is not shown on the album yet.

## Consequences
- **New:**
  - The table `place_regions` (`country` generated from `region`), in `20261017090000_stage4_atlas_regions.sql`, tested by `stage4_atlas_regions.test.sql`. **Apply it to the remote project before deploying.**
  - `src/core/atlas-regions.ts` (with tests), `src/core/regions.ts` (generated) and `searchRegions` in `src/core/atlas.ts`.
  - `POST /api/places/regions` (600 an hour).
  - `src/components/atlas/region-map.tsx` and `regions-view.tsx`, and the regions row in the country sheet.
  - The country card: `CardAtlas.regions`. The server keeps it to the owner's marked regions and the country's real count and kind.
  - Analytics `region_saved` `{ visited, via: "map" | "list" }`, never the region.
- **Data quirks we accept:**
  - Natural Earth's admin-1 is a few years old: Iran's and Vietnam's newest splits and mergers aren't there.
  - Kinds are approximate (Korea's provinces and metropolitan cities are "regions").
  - The world map's disputed borders and the regions' are both Natural Earth's default view.
- **Not built:**
  - Regions on the public album.
  - A first-visit year per region.
  - Cities.

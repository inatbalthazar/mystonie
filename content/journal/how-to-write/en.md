This is a **draft**: it shows on your computer and on previews, never on the live site. Every article is one Markdown file, and this one uses everything the Journal knows.

## Titles with an Add button

Put a title on its own line and it becomes a card. **Add** opens quick add on it, so a reader can save it in one tap.

@[movie:603](The Matrix)

@[series:1396](Breaking Bad)

## Lists and quotes

Three things that make a good list:

1. A clear reason to read it (*"finish in a weekend"*).
2. Titles as cards, so people can add them.
3. One line on why each one is here.

> Finishing is the best part.

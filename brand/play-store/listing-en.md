## Full description (2,157 of 4,000)

```text
Finished it? Mystonie it.

Mystonie is a collector's album for everything you finish: shows, movies, books, manga and games. Log it in a few taps, and every finish becomes a card worth sharing, with your rating, your stats and a FINISHED stamp.

LOG EVERY EPISODE, CHAPTER AND LEVEL
• Tick off episodes as you go, with the next one waiting on Home
• Keep your page in a book and your chapter in a manga
• Note how long a game took to beat
• Save what you want to watch, read or play next
• Bring your history over from Letterboxd, Goodreads, MyAnimeList or TV Time, and export it as a CSV any time

CARDS WORTH SHARING
• Ticket, Polaroid, Bold Stats, book spines, manga panels, game cartridges and more
• Sized for Stories and for feeds, in colours taken from the poster
• Rare finishes show how few people on Mystonie have finished the same title

YOUR YEAR IN BIG NUMBERS
• Watch, reading and play time, titles finished, your busiest month and the directors you keep coming back to
• A recap every week and every month, milestones as you hit them, and a Year in Review to share

SEE THE WORLD FROM YOUR COUCH
• The Atlas colours in every country your stories come from
• Mark the places you've been, lived and want to go, region by region

STICKERS FOR EVERY MILESTONE
• Your first movie, three in one day, ten series, a week of Reels in a row: 35 stickers to collect

BETTER WITH FRIENDS
• Follow friends and stamp their finishes in your feed
• Join fandom clubs and monthly challenges, and see who tops this week's board
• Guess the Reel of the Day: a new movie every day, six tries

KNOW BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY
• Pick the topics you'd rather avoid, like a dog dying or jump scares, and titles with them are flagged before you start
• Warnings come from DoesTheDogDie.com and from people who watched, down to the scene

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Works offline and syncs when you're back
• Dark mode for late-night logging
• In English and Thai
• Free, with no ads

Movie and TV data from TMDB. This app uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Game data from RAWG, streaming info from JustWatch and content warnings from DoesTheDogDie.com.
```

## Before you paste
- Everything above is in the app today. Pro isn't mentioned because it isn't on sale. Once it is, the Android app has to sell it through Google Play Billing (Google's payments policy), not Stripe.
- "Free, with no ads" is a promise. Take the line out if that might change.
- Letterboxd, Goodreads, MyAnimeList and TV Time are named once, for the import. Play allows other apps' names when they state a fact like this. Take the sentence out if a review ever objects.
